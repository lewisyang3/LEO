HALCYON PAYROLL SERVICES, INC.
PAYROLL AND BENEFITS ADMINISTRATION SERVICES AGREEMENT

Agreement No. HPS-3Y-0092

This Services Agreement is between Halcyon Payroll Services, Inc. ("Halcyon") and Brightwater Coffee Co. ("Client").

1. SERVICES

Halcyon will provide payroll processing, tax filing and benefits administration for up to 50 Client employees across all Client locations.

2. TERM

2.1 Effective Date. This Agreement is effective January 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date").

2.2 Term. This Agreement has a term of thirty-six (36) months from the Effective Date.

3. FEES

3.1 Monthly Service Fee. Client will pay a Monthly Service Fee of $2,400.00, billed on the 3rd day of each month.

3.2 Annual Adjustment. On each anniversary of the Effective Date, the Monthly Service Fee will increase by three percent (3%).

3.3 Per-employee overage. Each employee above 50 is billed at $45.00 per employee per month.

4. INVOICE DISPUTES

Client must notify Halcyon of any disputed charge at support@halcyonpayroll.example within sixty (60) days of the invoice date.
