MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

MSA No. KCH-MSA-2209

This Master Services Agreement is made between Kestrel Cloud Hosting, LLC ("Kestrel") and Brightwater Coffee Co. ("Customer").

1. SERVICES

1.1 Kestrel will provide managed hosting for Customer's online ordering website and point-of-sale integrations.

1.2 Service Start Date: September 1, 2024.

2. FEES

2.1 Monthly Fee. Managed Hosting - Standard: $1,200.00 per month, billed on the first day of each month.

2.2 Renewal Price Protection. Fees for any renewal term shall not exceed the fees for the immediately preceding term by more than five percent (5%).

3. TERM

3.1 Initial Term. The initial term is twenty-four (24) months from the Service Start Date.

3.2 Renewal. After the initial term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month renewal terms unless either party provides sixty (60) days' written notice of non-renewal.

4. BILLING DISPUTES

4.1 Customer may dispute any invoice in writing to billing@kestrelcloud.example within ninety (90) days of the invoice date. Kestrel will credit any amounts billed in error on the next invoice.
