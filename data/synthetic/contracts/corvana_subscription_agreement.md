CORVANA, INC.
SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

Agreement No. CRV-2025-0418

This Subscription Agreement is entered into between Corvana, Inc. ("Corvana") and Brightwater Coffee Co. ("Customer").

1. SUBSCRIPTION

1.1 Service. Corvana will provide Customer access to the Corvana CRM platform, Professional plan, for up to 12 named users.

1.2 Effective Date. This Agreement is effective as of December 16, 2025 (the "Effective Date").

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

2.1 Subscription Fee. The Professional plan subscription fee is $14,400.00 per year, billed annually in advance.

2.2 Payment. Customer authorizes Corvana to charge the payment card on file on the first day of each subscription term.

3. TERM AND RENEWAL

3.1 Initial Term. The initial term begins on the Effective Date and ends on December 15, 2026.

3.2 Automatic Renewal. This Agreement will automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

3.3 Renewal Pricing. Renewal terms will be billed at Corvana's then-current list price for the Professional plan.

4. NOTICES

4.1 Notices of non-renewal must be sent in writing to notices@corvana.example and are effective upon receipt.

5. GENERAL

5.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.
